The landing page

A lead-filter page built for cold Meta ad traffic.

This page converts a curious pre-retiree into a qualified strategy session application. It qualifies by investable assets and retirement timeline before they ever reach your calendar.

Why not send them to the FourStar website?

The website is designed for existing clients and people who already know the firm. Cold traffic from a Meta ad is a different audience. They need one focused message, one clear CTA, and a qualification form that filters for the right fit before the call. The landing page does all three. The website does none of them.

What makes this page convert?

It leads with the pain pre-retirees actually feel, not the services FourStar offers. It uses the Build, Live, Share three-phase framework to differentiate FourStar from every other RIA in Chicago. It qualifies by investable assets and timeline so the only people who fill out the form are ones worth talking to. And it positions the strategy session as a discovery conversation, not a sales call.

How the funnel works

Five steps from cold scroll to qualified strategy session.

1

Meta ad shown

One of the 9 ads runs on Facebook or Instagram, targeting Chicagoland users aged 50 to 70 with financial behavior signals. The ad interrupts the scroll with a message the avatar instantly recognizes as being about them.

2

Landing page

The ad click lands on the FourStar landing page. The page confirms the positioning from the ad, presents the three-phase philosophy, builds credibility with credentials and the Advisory HQ ranking, and ends with a qualifying form.

3

Qualification form

The form asks for investable assets, retirement timeline, and primary concern. Bad-fit leads self-select out. Only pre-retirees with $250K+ who are within 10 years of retirement and have a specific concern move forward.

4

Follow-up

FourStar receives the qualified application by email within minutes. The prospect is contacted within one business day to schedule the strategy session. They arrive already educated on the firm and the three-phase approach.

5

Strategy session

The conversation starts warm. The prospect has watched the video, read the page, and self-qualified by filling out the form. Brian or a FourStar advisor reviews their full picture and outlines the coordinated strategy. No cold intro needed.
