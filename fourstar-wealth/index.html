Script 2: The Contractor Who Finally Slept

Result and Case Study

Central angle

Skilled trades professional or business owner who built wealth through hard work but had no investment structure

Body (11-90s)

He'd built real wealth. But he'd spent his whole career focused on the next job, the next payroll, the next project. He never had time to think about how to turn what he'd built into a retirement plan.
