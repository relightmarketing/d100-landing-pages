About K+B Builders Inc.

Building Tampa Bay Since 2004

K+B Builders Inc. is a licensed and insured design-build firm founded in 2004. We bring designers, architects, and subcontractors together as a unified team. That means your designer, architect, and builder work side-by-side, providing you the convenience and confidence of a single point of contact for your entire project.

Our strategy for success is collaborating with each owner, architect, and design consultant from the initial concept through completion. This partnership approach optimizes the fit between design and budget, allowing us to forecast ahead of schedule and select the most qualified specialty trades.
