Your Search in Three Steps

Step 01
Brief and Search Strategy

Your dedicated Senior Consultant conducts a thorough needs analysis and position profiling. Together you define the ideal candidate profile, the team dynamics, and the non-negotiables. This is not a 15-minute intake call. It is the foundation of the entire search.

Step 02
Screen and Present

Proactive sourcing via the consultant's own industry network and multi-channel strategy. Every candidate is personally screened and interviewed before you see their profile. You receive a shortlist of best-fit candidates, not a stack of CVs.

Step 03
Place and Follow Up

Offer negotiation support, onboarding guidance, and ongoing follow-up after placement. Your consultant stays involved to make sure the new hire integrates into the team and delivers results from day one. The relationship does not end at signing.
