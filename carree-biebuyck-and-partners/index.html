Contrarian / Insight

+

Central Angle

The fundamental difference between executive search and recruitment is whether a senior partner does the work or a junior consultant does, and that difference determines whether your hire stays.

Hook (0-10s)

Every executive search firm promises the same thing. Tailored approach. Deep network. Confidential process. But here is the question nobody asks: who actually does the work? Because the answer to that question determines everything.

Body (11-90s)

At most search firms, here is what really happens. A senior partner wins the mandate. They do the intake meeting. They shake your hand. And then they hand the file to a team of junior consultants and researchers who have never run a business, never sat on a board, and never had to make a leadership decision under pressure.

Those junior consultants search databases, screen CVs, and compile a longlist. By the time a candidate reaches your desk, the person who understood your business, your culture, and your strategic priorities is three steps removed from the actual search.

That is not executive search. That is recruitment with a premium fee.

At Carree Biebuyck and Partners, the senior partner who wins your trust is the same person who maps the market, assesses candidates, and presents the shortlist. Five partners. Three offices in Belgium. Every search handled at the highest level.

That is why 90% of our placements stay and succeed. When someone who truly understands your company selects the candidates, the fit is not a guess. It is a judgment call made by someone qualified to make it.
