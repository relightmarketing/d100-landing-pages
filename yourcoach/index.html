Script 1 - "Feeling Stuck Is Not the Problem" (Pain)

Hook (0-10s)

If you feel stuck in your job, your first instinct is to update your CV and start looking for something new. But here is the thing: if you do not understand why you feel stuck, you will end up stuck again in the next job too.

Body (11-90s)

I have coached hundreds of people who came to me saying "I need a new job." And almost every time, after a few sessions, we discover it was never about the job. It was about values that were not being honored. Skills they were not using. A version of themselves they had put aside to fit in.

That is what real coaching uncovers. Not just "what do you want to do next," but "who are you when you are at your best, and how do we build your life around that?"

At YourCoach, we take a creatief en activerend approach. That means you will not just sit and talk for an hour. You will move, draw, experiment, and discover things about yourself that no personality test would ever reveal. Our coaches are ICF-certified, and they operate across all of Flanders and Brussels. But more importantly, they are people who have walked their own path and know what transformation actually requires.

Call to action (90-120s)

If you are ready to stop guessing and start understanding, click the link below. Book a free intake conversation. No pressure. Just clarity on what is actually going on and what your options are.

Filming brief: Seated in a bright, creative space. Plants, natural light, warm atmosphere. Phone propped at eye-level, direct to camera. Medium shot, chest and above. Warm, empathetic energy, slightly provocative at the opening, then reassuring. Smart casual, warm colors (rust, teal, olive). Avoid black suits.
Script 2 - "What Our Graduates Actually Do" (Result)

Hook (0-10s)

Most coach training programs give you a certificate and wish you luck. We give you skills you can use the next day, a community that keeps growing with you, and a foundation that meets international ICF standards.

Body (11-90s)

Our Integrale Coaching program is not a weekend workshop. It is a deep, transformative experience that changes how you see yourself and how you work with others. Our graduates go on to build their own coaching practices, become team coaches in organizations, lead leadership development programs, and bring coaching skills into their existing careers.

What makes the difference is our approach. We do not just teach models and frameworks. We practice them. In every session, you coach and get coached. You work with your body, your creativity, and your emotions. Van hoofd naar lijf. That is how real coaching skill is built.

And when the program ends, you do not walk away alone. YourCoach has a community of graduates who meet regularly for intervision, practice, and support. That is what turns a certificate into a career.

Call to action (90-120s)

If you want to see whether this path is right for you, click the link below. Book a free conversation with us. We will talk through your goals, the program, and whether it is the right fit. No obligation. Just an honest conversation.

Filming brief: Standing in the coaching loft, training materials visible in background. Phone propped at eye-level. Medium shot. Natural light. Confident, passionate about the program, community-oriented energy. Smart casual, warm tones.
Script 3 - "Why Reading Another Self-Help Book Won't Work" (Contrarian)

Hook (0-10s)

You have read the books. You have taken the personality tests. You might have even tried meditation for three weeks. And you still feel exactly the same. Here is why none of that works on its own.

Body (11-90s)

Self-help resources are great for awareness. They help you name what is going on. But naming it and changing it are two completely different skills. You can read every book on confidence and still freeze in a meeting. You can understand your attachment style and still struggle in relationships.

The missing piece is practice with a real person who holds a mirror up to you, challenges your patterns, and helps you build new ones in real time. That is what coaching does that books cannot.

At YourCoach, our coaching is niet alleen praten. We use creative techniques, physical movement, and embodied exercises because transformation does not happen in your head alone. It happens when your body learns a new pattern too. Our coaches are certified, experienced, and genuinely invested in your growth.

Call to action (90-120s)

If you are done reading and ready to start doing, click the link below. Book a free intake conversation. We will talk about what you have tried, what you actually need, and how coaching can help. No pressure. Just a real conversation.

Filming brief: Seated casually in a warm creative space. Phone propped at eye-level. Medium shot. Natural light. Conversational, slightly provocative energy, then warm and inviting. Smart casual, warm tones.
