Script 3 - Your Lawyer Should Think Like a Business Owner (Contrarian / Insight)

Hook

Here is something most law firms will never tell you. The technically perfect legal answer is not always the right business decision. If your lawyer gives you advice without considering the commercial impact, they are solving the wrong problem.

Body

I see it all the time. A business owner asks for advice on a dispute. The lawyer maps out every legal option in perfect detail. Six weeks of analysis. A 40-page memo. And at the end, the business owner still does not know what to actually do, because nobody asked the question that matters: what is the commercial outcome you need, and what is the fastest, most cost-effective path to get there? That is the difference between a lawyer who knows the law and a lawyer who understands business. At Reyns Advocaten, we start every conversation with the commercial reality. What is at stake financially? What is the timeline? What does a win actually look like for your company? Then we build the legal strategy around that. We call it een no-nonsense en pragmatische aanpak. It means we will tell you when fighting a case is worth it, and we will tell you when settling is smarter. Not because we want less work, but because your result is what matters, not our billable hours.
