CTA (90 - 120s)

"If you're tired of chasing contractors who ghost you, click the link below. Watch the short video on the next page, then fill out the form to schedule your free consultation. No pressure, just answers."

Filming brief

Environment: clean modern showroom or completed kitchen. Camera: eye-level medium shot with supporting b-roll. Framing: direct-to-camera for trust moments, wide portfolio cutaways for proof. Lighting: soft and professional. Energy: calm, empathetic, confident. Wardrobe: teal polo or dark neutral outfit with a tablet or cabinet sample as a prop.
