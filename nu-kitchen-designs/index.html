Meet Josh Torres

Nu Kitchen Designs is founded and operated by Josh Torres, a hands-on remodeling specialist with over 22 years of experience in home construction. Josh learned his craft working alongside master tradesmen early in his career, building expertise in every trade from framing to custom cabinetry.

Our Process

After years of experience in Orlando, we have outlined the most efficient process for a seamless home transformation. Six simple steps from consultation to completion.

1

Free Consultation

Virtual or in-person meeting to discuss your project, design aesthetic, timeline, and budget. No obligation, no pressure.

2

Measurements

Detailed, precise measurements of your entire space. Every quarter-inch matters for a perfect finished product.

3

3D Design and Review

See your new space in a full 3D virtual design. Adjust finishes, colors, and fixtures until everything is exactly right.

4

Ordering Materials

After you approve the design, we order all materials. Products and finishes begin arriving, and we prepare for installation.

5

Demo

We do not start demo until all materials arrive, so you have a usable space for as long as possible. Then we clear out the old and prepare for the new.

6

Installation

Our team installs cabinets, countertops, flooring, and fixtures efficiently to meet deadlines. Before you know it, your dream space is ready.
