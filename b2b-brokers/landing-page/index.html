Sound familiar?

Desperate to retire, but you can't?

  • "I am completely burned out." You pushed hard for decades, and the business still needs you every single day.
  • "I'm already 10 years the 'bottleneck' of my business." Estimating, scheduling, payables, receivables: it all still runs through you, and you know it.
  • "Any legit appraisal company I find wants 1000s of dollars." So you still have no real idea what the business is worth, or whether it is enough to retire on.
  • "A lot of tire kickers who waste your time." And every unvetted buyer conversation is a chance for employees or customers to find out.
  • "Businesses can sit on the market for years." Waiting "until I am ready" makes it worse, because an unprepared, owner-dependent business does not sell.
Here is the part nobody tells you: 70 to 80 percent of businesses that go to market never sell, per Exit Planning Institute data. Not because the owners did not want out, but because they started too late and the business could not run without them. The owners who get out on their own terms start planning 12 to 18 months ahead.

The exit-readiness approach

Make a plan, Work the plan

You have already seen what does not work: brokers who list and pray while the listing sits, expensive standalone appraisals, years grooming a successor who never takes over. Allen Stockbridge starts earlier and works backward from your exit.

  • Start 12 to 18 months out A proper exit is prepared, not listed: readiness work first, then valuation, marketing, due diligence, and closing.
  • Fix owner dependence first If the business cannot run without you, it will not sell, so the plan addresses that before buyers ever see it.
  • Know the value "as is", then improve it You learn what the business is worth today and exactly what can be done to position it for the exit you want.
