Common Questions

What patients ask before booking

Is All-on-4 really permanent?

All-on-4 implants remain fixed in your mouth. They are not removable like traditional dentures. Most patients find them indistinguishable from natural teeth in everyday use.

What does the investment look like?

The cost of dental implants is higher than traditional dentures, but flexible financing and monthly payment options are available. The consult is where you get a real number for your specific situation.

How do I know if I am a candidate?

Most adults with missing teeth, failing teeth, or unstable dentures are candidates. The implant consult confirms your fit. Coming in without knowing the answer is completely normal and expected.
