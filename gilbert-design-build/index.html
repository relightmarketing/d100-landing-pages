Our Proven 6-Step Process

One contract. One point of contact. One accountable team working to make your remodeling project easier and more enjoyable than you ever imagined.

1

Design Center Meeting

Visit our Lakewood Ranch showroom to discuss your project goals, timeline, and budget with our team.

2

Designer Home Visit

Your consultant visits your home to review the space, gather measurements, and understand your vision in person.

3

Design Selection

Return to our showroom to review 3D renderings, make product selections, and refine every design detail with your consultant.

4

Construction Agreement

Review and approve the final plan, including the full scope of work, product selections, and transparent pricing. No surprises.

5

Pre-Start Meeting

Meet your project manager on-site to review access, work hours, and what to expect throughout the construction phase.

6

Enjoy the Result

Your dream remodel is complete. Backed by a two-year workmanship warranty, enjoy your beautiful new space for years to come.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions Sarasota homeowners have about the remodeling process.

Design-build means one company handles both the design and construction of your remodel. At Gilbert Design Build, this gives you a single point of contact, a seamless process, and full accountability from concept to completion. There is no need to hire and manage separate designers and contractors, which saves time, reduces miscommunication, and typically delivers a shorter overall project timeline.
Kitchen remodels vary widely based on the scope of work, the size of the space, and the grade of finishes you choose. Because every project is unique, we recommend scheduling a free consultation so we can discuss your goals and provide a realistic estimate tailored to your vision and budget.
Many bathroom remodels in the Sarasota and Bradenton area fall between $35,000 and $90,000, depending on the scope of work and the size of the bathroom. A simple cosmetic refresh will be on the lower end, while a full gut renovation with structural changes will be higher. Contact us for a personalized estimate.
A bathroom remodel typically takes 3 to 4 weeks for straightforward renovations and 6 to 8 weeks for more extensive work. Kitchen remodels and whole-home projects take longer. The design process itself takes 3 to 6 months on average. We provide estimated timelines during the consultation so you know what to expect.
