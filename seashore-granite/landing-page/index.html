How It Works

From Showroom to Installed in Three Steps

01

Showroom Visit & Free Estimate

Visit our Sarasota showroom to browse over 30 brands of stone in full slab size. Walk through granite, quartz, marble, and porcelain under real lighting. Get a no-obligation estimate tailored to your project — or ask us to come to you for an in-home consultation.

02

Precise Measurement & Design

Once you've selected your stone, our team comes to your home for precision measuring. We plan seam placement, edge profiles, and layout together so there are no surprises on installation day. Every detail is confirmed before fabrication begins.

03

Fabrication, Demo & Installation

We handle demolition of your existing countertops, then fabricate and install the new stone — typically within two weeks of your measurement. Our crew does the full job: demo, haul-out, install, and cleanup. You walk into a finished kitchen.
