Script 1: The Hidden Price Tag

Pain / Circumstance

+

Central Angle

Most Sarasota homeowners have no clue what a new roof costs until a salesman is already sitting in their living room. Legacy lets you see the number before anyone shows up.

Hook (0-10s)

If you're a homeowner in Sarasota or Bradenton, let me ask you something. Do you know what a new roof actually costs? Not a guess. Not what your neighbor paid five years ago. The real number for your home, your roof, right now. Most people have no idea until a salesman is sitting at their table, and by then the pressure is already on.

Body (11-90s)

Here is what usually happens. You call a couple of roofing companies. They send someone out. That person walks your roof, sits down at your kitchen table, and hits you with a number you were not expecting. And now you are stuck, because you have no frame of reference. Is that price fair? Is it too high? Are they padding it because they think you do not know any better?

We built Legacy Roofing differently. Before anyone sets foot on your property, you can go to our website and use our roofing calculator. You plug in your details, and you see a real estimate. No appointment. No salesman. No pressure. Just the number.

And when we do come out for the free inspection, there are no surprises. The price we quoted is the price you pay. We had a homeowner sign a contract, and when our crew started the tear-off, they found the old roof had been torch-applied, which means extra work to remove it. We covered the additional cost because that is what accountability looks like. He said we did what we promised, when we promised. His new roof looks great.

That is how we operate. Accountability, Communication, Teamwork. We call it ACT. It is not a slogan. It is how every job runs.

CTA (90-120s)

Script 2: The Gamero Promise

Result / Case Study

+

A real homeowner's roof replacement hit a hidden problem during tear-off, and Legacy absorbed the extra cost rather than surprising the customer.

But that is not how we do things at Legacy. We gave that homeowner a price. We shook hands on it. And when the unexpected showed up, we handled it. The extra cost was minimal and we did not pass it along as a surprise invoice. He said we did what we promised, when we promised. His new roof looks great.

That is what we mean when we say Accountability, Communication, Teamwork. Every job gets a clear proposal upfront. Every crew communicates daily. We cover your landscaping, we clean up every single night, and when the job is done, there are no roofing nails in your driveway and no surprise charges in your inbox.

We have 518 reviews and a 4.6-star rating because we treat every home like it belongs to our own family. Not because we are perfect, but because when something goes wrong, we own it.

Script 3: Why "Get Three Quotes" Fails

Contrarian / Insight

+

Central Angle

The standard advice to "get three quotes and pick the middle one" is exactly why homeowners end up overpaying or hiring the wrong crew.

Hook (0-10s)

If you need a new roof, everyone is going to tell you the same thing. Get three quotes and go with the middle one. Sounds smart, right? It is actually the reason most homeowners end up overpaying or hiring the wrong crew.

Body (11-90s)

Here is the problem. When you get three quotes for a roof, you are almost never comparing the same thing. One company quotes 25-year shingles, another quotes 30-year architectural. One includes a full tear-off, another is layering over the old roof. One has a 5-year labor warranty, another has 15 years. The prices look different because the jobs are different. And most homeowners do not know enough about roofing to spot the difference.

So what happens? You pick the cheapest one because it seems like a deal, or you pick the middle one because it feels safe. Either way, you are guessing.

We decided to fix that. At Legacy Roofing, you can use our online roofing calculator before you talk to anyone. It gives you a real estimate based on your roof. And when we come out for the free inspection, we walk you through exactly what is included: the materials, the timeline, the warranty, the cleanup process. No vague line items. No "we will figure it out when we get up there."

We back our work with up to 50 years on materials and 15 years on labor. We are licensed in seven Florida counties and we have a 94% recommendation rate from verified homeowners. When we say no surprises, we mean it.

CTA (90-120s)

Stop guessing what your roof should cost. Click the link below, use the free roofing calculator, and see your real number in two minutes. If it makes sense, book a free inspection. No commitment. No pressure. Just clarity.

Environment: Outdoors on a residential Sarasota street with rooftops visible
Camera: Phone on mini-tripod, eye level, direct to camera
Framing: Medium shot, chest and above, rooflines visible behind
Lighting: Natural late-afternoon Florida light, golden hour ideal
Energy: Slightly contrarian and confident. Educational in the body, inviting in the CTA.
Wardrobe: Sky blue or burnt orange polo matching Legacy brand palette. Avoid all-black or all-white.
