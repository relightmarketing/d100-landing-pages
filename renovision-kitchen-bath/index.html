How it works

3 Steps. Zero Surprises.

Our process is designed to eliminate guesswork. You will see, approve, and experience every detail of your remodel before construction begins.

Step 01

Home Visit and Laser Measurement

We come to your home with professional laser-sensitive measuring devices. Every dimension of your kitchen or bathroom is captured with precision, not estimated with a tape measure and a notepad.

Step 02

Computer Design and VR Preview

We build a complete 3D model of your new space using the laser measurements. Then you visit our showroom, put on VR goggles, and walk through your finished kitchen or bathroom. Every cabinet, countertop, and fixture, visible and accurate.

Step 03

Build and Reveal

Once you have approved the design, our team handles every phase of construction. You already know exactly what the finished result will look like, because you have already walked through it.
