Video scripts

Central angle

If your last contractor ghosted, did shoddy work, or turned your home into a construction zone for months, the problem was not that you made a bad choice. The problem is that most remodeling companies are not set up to protect you. Revive is.

Hook

If your last kitchen or bathroom contractor left you with a half-finished project, broken promises, and a mess in your home, this is for you. You are not alone. We hear this story every single week from homeowners across Tampa and Orlando.

Body

Most people wait after a bad contractor experience, not because of timing or budget, but because they do not want to go through the same chaos again. Revive's differentiator is that everything runs under one roof, one team, one project manager, one warranty, instead of five disconnected trades and a homeowner stuck in the middle.

CTA

Click the link, see the short page, fill out the form, and schedule a complimentary in-home consultation with no pressure.

Filming brief

Film in a bright completed kitchen or showroom. Keep the tone empathetic and confident. Phone on a tripod, eye-level, natural light, navy polo or button-down.

Central angle

A homeowner on his fourth kitchen remodel met with multiple companies before choosing Revive. He wanted real wood and dovetail drawers, not a cheap big-box kitchen, and he found the most cost-efficient option with the strongest process clarity.

Hook

“I do not want a cheap Home Depot like kitchen. I want real wood and dovetail drawers.” He had already met with multiple kitchen companies. None of them gave him what he wanted at a price that made sense.

Body

The story proves that premium does not have to mean vague or overpriced. Revive wins by helping the homeowner understand exactly what they are paying for, how the materials differ, and why the process feels more secure from day one.

CTA

Click through, walk through the process, fill out the short form, and get a detailed in-home consultation with pricing clarity.

Filming brief

Central angle

Hiring separate trades and managing them yourself looks cheaper on paper, but the delays, finger-pointing, and stress usually make it more expensive in both money and sanity.

Hook

If you are thinking about hiring a plumber, an electrician, a tile guy, and a cabinet installer separately, let me save you six months of headaches. That approach does not save you money. It costs you more.

Body

The real enemy is fragmented accountability. Revive removes that by keeping the trades, the project manager, the communication, and the warranty under one company and one schedule.
